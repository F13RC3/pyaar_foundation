About Pyaar Foundation

" पशु रक्शनये सदैव तत्पर "

With the vision and mission of Pashu Rakhshanay Sadaiv tatpar - Petanity and animal rehabilitators Foundation _ chandrapur was founded in 2018 with an aim to save and serve every needy street animal to get a second chance to live a happy life.

HUMAN INVOLVEMENT

On the counter part Pyaar foundation always succeeded in reducing human-animal conflict by generating awareness on various animal issues . On an average every year thousands of lives get a new quality life to exist at our rehabilitation shelter
